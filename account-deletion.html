Delete your Disguys account

Developer: Otilab Yazılım Anonim Şirketi

You can request deletion of your Disguys account and associated game data by email without installing or opening the game.

  1. Email info@otilab.com with the subject Disguys account deletion request.
  2. If known, include your Disguys Player ID, display name, and sign-in method: Guest / Google Play Games / Apple Game Center.
  3. If you do not know your Player ID, ask support for help identifying your account.

Account ownership must be verified before deletion. Knowing a Player ID alone does not authorize deletion.

We do not ask for your password, access token, refresh token, or Game Center authentication signature. Do not include these in your email.

This request concerns your Disguys account and associated game data. It does not delete your Google or Apple account.

Deletion scope, processing time, and retention

The specific data categories to be deleted, the processing time for verified requests, and whether any data must be retained (and for how long) have not yet been determined.
