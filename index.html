About / Overview

Everyone is suspicious.

DISGUYS is a cross-platform prop hunt game where you can play online with friends—or fill the match with bots when you play solo.

Enjoy online multiplayer with up to 12 players across mobile, PC, and console. If you prefer to play by yourself, bots can join as team-mates or opponents, with easy and hard difficulty options.

Choose between different map styles in three different sizes. Every match divides players into two teams: Sniffers hunt, while Stooges transform into objects and hide.

Seek. Aim. Eliminate.

Sniffers

Find and hunt down the opposing team. Aim and shoot to kill, but spend every round carefully—when a Sniffer runs out of ammo, they die.

Win by hunting every Stooge
Transform. Hide. Survive.

Stooges

Aim at an object and shoot to transform into it. Shoot without a target to return to human form, switch props while disguised, and turn invisible for five seconds when escape is your only option.

One survivor means victory
About / How it plays

Read the room.

The environment is your disguise, your danger and your best chance to outplay the other team.

01 / Disguise

Become the room.

Transform

Stooges shoot at target objects to copy them, and can transform again while already in prop form.

Read the targets

Not every item is targetable. Objects you can transform into are highlighted with a green light effect.

02 / Escape

Break the chase.

Disappear briefly

Turn invisible for five seconds to lose the hunter. The skill needs time to refresh before it can be used again.

Grab a booster

Boosters spawn randomly around the map and grant different effects. A player can carry only one at a time.

03 / Replay

Change every hunt.

Fill the lobby

Play solo with AI team-mates and opponents, then choose easy or hard bots to match the challenge you want.

Learn new maps

Choose between two visual themes and three map sizes to reshape routes, hiding spaces and match pressure.
